# t8: AI disclosure in merged PRs by year and half-year

Produced by `scripts/03h_ai_disclosure.py` (reuses 03c's frozen term lists and `author_class`). Files: `t8_ai_disclosure_by_year.csv`, `t8_ai_disclosure_by_half.csv`. Precision verdicts: `data/pr_text_ai_samples_full.csv` (column `round`). Deviation log: METHODOLOGY D15d.

## Definitions (lower bound, never an adoption rate)
- Population: merged PRs in high-confidence repos (D14), created 2020-01-01 or later, dated by creation. Bots excluded. AI agent accounts (copilot, copilot-swe-agent, devin-ai-integration, cursor, codegen-sh, claude) are their own class.
- `human_with_signal` / `share_human_signal`: human merged PRs whose title or body matches the `claude` author group, after the negation line filter. Only groups passing the D15 gate (>= 80%) are used; only `claude` passes. PR title and body only, not commits.
- `median_dpg_share_dpgs_ge20_prs`: median across DPGs with at least 20 merged human PRs in the period (`n_dpgs_ge20_prs` of them) of that DPG's share. Shows the typical DPG, not the pooled total.
- `share_ai_agent_of_human_plus_agent`: agent-authored merged PRs / (human + agent merged PRs).
- `dpgs_with_disclosed_ai_pr`, `dpgs_with_ai_agent_pr`: DPGs with at least one such merged PR. `dpgs_active`: DPGs with any merged non-bot PR.
- `top6_repo_share_of_signal`: share of the period's signal PRs coming from the six repos with most signal PRs.
- Disclosure is voluntary and norms/templates changed, so a rise is partly more disclosure. 2026 is partial (to 2026-10-29); 2026H2 = Jul 1 to Oct 29 (about 4 of 6 months).

## Negation filter
Before matching, lines stating AI was NOT used are dropped (regex list in the script; D15d). It removed 129 matches, all in qgis/QGIS in 2026 (the template line "I certify that no AI tool(s) (Copilot, Claude, ...) were used"). Other repos: 0 removed.

## Precision gate (D15)
Round 1 (before the fix; seed 2029; human merged PRs since 2024):
| group | correct / n | precision | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| claude | 25/30 | 83% | pass |
| generic | 20/30 | 67% | excluded |
| copilot | 16/28 | 57% | excluded |
| chatgpt_codex | 10/30 | 33% | excluded |
| other_tool | 5/30 | 17% | excluded |
| cursor | 0/1 | n/a | excluded |

Round 2 (claude only, after the negation fix; fresh draw, seed 2030, 30 of 4,141 matches since 2024): **24/30 = 80%**, passes at the threshold. The 6 errors: 4 unticked template option lists (OpenFn "I have used Claude Code / another model / not used AI"), 1 conditional checklist item (opensupplyhub), 1 reference to the CLAUDE.md config file. The sample is small: the 95% interval on 80% is roughly 62-91%.
Borderline rules: a PR description "generated by Copilot" counted correct (round 1); unticked template text counted incorrect.

## Concentration (claude signal, 2026)
4,052 signal PRs across 70 repos. Six repos give 54.7%: QGIS 592, polis 449, kolibri 401, OpenFn lightning 276, Altinn 249, eheza-app 249. But 69 DPGs have at least one disclosed PR in 2026 and 35 repos have 10 or more, so it is not one project. The OpenFn template false positives (unticked options) are the main remaining contamination; no per-repo cap is applied. The median-DPG column is the better guide to the typical DPG.
